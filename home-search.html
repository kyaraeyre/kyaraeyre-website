FAQ

Frequently asked questions about custom home searches

How does a custom home search with Kyara work?

You share what you're looking for, price range, area, property type, must-haves, and Kyara personally sets up a search in the MLS matched to your criteria and reaches out to walk you through next steps.

Is there a cost to use this service?

No. Setting up a custom search and working with Kyara as a buyer's agent costs nothing out of pocket to you as a buyer.

How is this different from browsing listing sites myself?

Your search is built and monitored directly against the MLS by a local expert, so you see accurate, timely listings, including ones that fit your criteria closely, without sifting through irrelevant results.

How quickly will Kyara follow up?

Kyara typically reaches out within one business day of receiving your search request.

Can I update my search criteria later?

Yes. Your search is a starting point. Kyara will refine it with you as you see listings and get a clearer sense of what you want.

Prefer to just talk it through?

Reach out directly and Kyara will walk you through your options.
