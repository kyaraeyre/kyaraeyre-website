How much does a luxury home in North Scottsdale cost in 2026?

By Kyara Eyre · Published August 24, 2026 · Williams Luxury Homes

What is the price range for a luxury home in North Scottsdale in 2026?

North Scottsdale luxury homes generally run from about $1 million to $30 million or more. That's a wide band, and where a specific property lands within it depends heavily on which community it's in, lot size, view corridor, and whether it includes golf club access.

Entry-level North Scottsdale properties, including some homes in DC Ranch and Grayhawk, sit toward the lower end of that range, while guard-gated estates in Silverleaf and Desert Mountain regularly reach into eight figures.

How does price vary across North Scottsdale's communities?

Price varies significantly by community. DC Ranch and Grayhawk offer some of the broadest price ranges in North Scottsdale, making them a common starting point for buyers new to the area. Troon North and Estancia sit in the middle to upper range, often tied to golf course frontage or larger desert-view lots.

Silverleaf and Desert Mountain represent the top tier of the market: Silverleaf for its guard-gated, architecturally distinct estates, and Desert Mountain for its scale, privacy, and private golf club infrastructure. Buyers comparing communities should ask for current closed comparables in each, since list price alone can be misleading in a market this varied.

What drives higher prices in specific North Scottsdale neighborhoods?

Three factors tend to move price the most in North Scottsdale: proximity to TPC Scottsdale (home of the WM Phoenix Open), direct access to McDowell Sonoran Preserve trailheads, and guard-gated privacy. Properties that combine two or more of these (for example, a Silverleaf estate near preserve trail access) typically command a premium over otherwise comparable homes.

Golf course frontage inside a private club community is another consistent driver, particularly in Desert Mountain and Troon North. The presence of frontage alone doesn't guarantee membership access, though; that's typically a separate arrangement with the club.

What should buyers budget for beyond the purchase price?

Beyond the purchase price, buyers in gated and golf communities should budget for HOA dues and, where applicable, separate golf or social club membership costs and initiation fees. These figures vary by community and by club, and should be confirmed directly with the HOA or club before writing an offer, since they can meaningfully affect the total cost of ownership.

How can buyers get accurate, current pricing?

The most reliable way to price a specific North Scottsdale property is a comparative market analysis pulled from recent, comparable closed sales in that exact community, not a Valley-wide average, which can be misleading given how much price varies block to block here. A local agent with current MLS access and relationships across these communities can typically turn one around within a day or two.

Frequently asked questions

Which North Scottsdale community has the highest home prices?

Silverleaf and Desert Mountain are generally the highest-priced North Scottsdale communities, known for guard-gated estates and, in Desert Mountain's case, private golf club membership.

Are DC Ranch and Grayhawk more affordable than Silverleaf?

Generally yes. DC Ranch and Grayhawk offer a wider range of price points within North Scottsdale, while Silverleaf and Desert Mountain skew toward the higher end of the market.

Do North Scottsdale golf communities require club membership?

Some do and some don't. Membership structures vary by community and club, so buyers should confirm current membership requirements and costs directly with each club before purchasing.
