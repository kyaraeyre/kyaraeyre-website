WLH Studios

Your home, turned into a real estate movie.

Marketing and video production, in-house, online and print. I discover your property's story, then WLH Studios shoots and edits it, content built to stop the scroll and capture the right buyer's attention.

  • Photography. Magazine-quality professional photography, including twilight and lifestyle images.
  • Cinematic video. Elegantly produced, high-definition property films with scripted storytelling.
  • Aerial & drone. Drone footage and aerial flyovers that show the setting, the views, and the location.

For certain properties, I'll also bring in one of my preferred outside media partners, a small group of pros I trust completely, so every listing gets exactly the production it deserves.
