Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026. This page explains what kyaraeyre.com collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

What information does this site collect?

This site collects information you provide directly through the contact form on contact.html, including your name, email address, phone number, and message. This site does not use a database; form submissions are handled by a third-party form service or forwarded by email, as configured by Kyara Eyre. No information is collected automatically beyond standard, non-identifying web server logs (such as browser type and page requested) unless you accept analytics cookies as described below.

How is the contact form handled?

When you submit the contact form, your information is sent to Kyara Eyre or a form-handling service acting on her behalf, solely to respond to your inquiry about buying, selling, or valuing a property. It is not sold, and it is not used for purposes unrelated to your inquiry without your separate consent.

What cookies does this site use?

On your first visit, a banner offers the choice to accept or decline non-essential cookies. If you decline, only strictly necessary cookies (such as remembering your cookie choice itself) are used. If you accept, this site may load analytics cookies to help understand how visitors use the site. As of this policy's last update, no analytics or advertising scripts are installed on this site. The acceptance mechanism exists so that any such tool added in the future loads only after you consent.

Does this site use analytics or advertising trackers?

Not at this time. If analytics (such as Google Analytics) or advertising pixels (such as a Meta Pixel) are added later, they will load only after a visitor accepts non-essential cookies through the banner described above, and this policy will be updated to name the specific tools in use.

Do third-party links on this site have their own privacy practices?

Yes. This site links to third-party profiles and platforms, including Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok. Each of those platforms has its own privacy policy governing information collected once you leave this site, and this policy does not cover their practices.

What choices do you have?

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which will cause the consent banner to reappear. You can also request that Kyara Eyre delete contact information you've submitted by emailing the address below.

Who can you contact with privacy questions?
