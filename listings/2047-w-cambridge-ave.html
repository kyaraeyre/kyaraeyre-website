What is it like to live in Encanto Estates in Phoenix?

Encanto Estates is a central Phoenix neighborhood near 19th Avenue and Thomas Road, close to the historic Encanto Park area and about 10 minutes from downtown Phoenix. It is a convenient home base for buyers who want easy access to Brophy and Xavier College Preparatory, ASU Downtown, Grand Canyon University, and downtown's restaurants and nightlife, on a lot with more room to spread out than most infill Phoenix neighborhoods offer.
