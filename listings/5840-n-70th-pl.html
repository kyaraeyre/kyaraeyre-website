What is the Hidden Village neighborhood like?

This home sits in Hidden Village, a small enclave in Paradise Valley near Scottsdale Road and McDonald Drive, close to the base of Camelback Mountain and the Sanctuary and El Chorro dining corridor that anchors much of the town's day to day life. The location also puts Quail Run Road and Jackrabbit Road within easy reach, keeping the home well connected to the rest of Paradise Valley and neighboring Scottsdale. Paradise Valley is its own incorporated town between Phoenix and Scottsdale, known for large lot privacy rather than commercial density, with home prices generally running from about $2 million to $20 million or more depending on lot size and mountain views.
