What is it like to live near Doubletree Ranch Rd in Scottsdale?

Gainey Village sits near the corner of Scottsdale Road and Doubletree Ranch Road, in the heart of what the site calls Central Scottsdale, close to Gainey Ranch's resort grounds and the Hyatt Regency Scottsdale Resort. The location puts residents a short drive from Fashion Square, Old Town Scottsdale's dining and nightlife, and the Camelback corridor, while still offering the quiet of a guard-gated, cul-de-sac setting. Central Scottsdale spans a wide range of home types, from condos and townhomes to gated golf-course estates, generally pricing from about $300,000 to $7 million or more depending on subdivision and lot size. Frequently asked questions about 7380 E Vaquero Dr

How many bedrooms and bathrooms does this villa have?

7380 E Vaquero Dr has 3 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms (2 full, 1 half) across approximately 3,112 square feet, plus a den or fourth bedroom option.

Is there an HOA?

Yes. The Gainey Village Owners Association fee is $1,340 per quarter, about $446.67 per month, and covers front yard maintenance, street maintenance, cable, and the guard-gated entry and community amenities.

What school district is this villa in?

Does this villa have a private pool?
