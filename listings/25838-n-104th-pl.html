What is it like to live in Troon Fairways in Scottsdale?

Troon Fairways is a gated golf community in North Scottsdale near Happy Valley Road and Pima Road, with views toward Troon Mountain and Pinnacle Peak. The community offers a quieter, established setting within reach of North Scottsdale shopping, dining, and the hiking trails that make this part of the Valley a draw for buyers who want space, privacy, and mountain scenery close to home.
