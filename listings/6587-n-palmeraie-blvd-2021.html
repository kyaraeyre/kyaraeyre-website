What is it like to live near Lincoln Drive in Paradise Valley?

The Ritz-Carlton Residences sit along Lincoln Drive near Palmeraie Boulevard in Paradise Valley, close to the base of Camelback Mountain and within easy reach of the Sanctuary and El Chorro dining corridor that anchors much of the town's day to day life. The location also puts Old Town Scottsdale and Biltmore area shopping and dining within a short drive. Paradise Valley is its own incorporated town between Phoenix and Scottsdale, known for large lot privacy and guard-gated communities rather than commercial density, with home prices generally running from about $2 million to $20 million or more depending on the property.
