About Kyara

Meet Kyara Eyre, your personal connection to luxury Real Estate throughout Scottsdale & Paradise Valley

An Arizona-licensed Realtor® (2022) with Williams Luxury Homes, working with buyers and sellers throughout the Phoenix Valley, with particular focus in Paradise Valley, North Scottsdale, Central Scottsdale, Arcadia, and North Phoenix.

Arizona Real Estate License #SA703476000

What is Kyara's real estate background?

Kyara Eyre became an Arizona-licensed Realtor® in 2022 and joined Williams Luxury Homes that same year, building $7.4M in sales volume since. She represents buyers and sellers throughout the greater Phoenix Valley, with particular focus in Paradise Valley, North Scottsdale, Central Scottsdale, Arcadia, and North Phoenix.

What matters most in how Kyara works with clients?

Real estate is personal to me, especially at the luxury level, and I think the strongest client relationships are built on trust and real connection. I want to know my clients well enough to understand what actually matters to them, so I can advocate for them at every step of a transaction.

I'm relationship driven and detail oriented: proactive updates, timely reporting, and an experience I want to feel easy and memorable while I deliver serious results. I also think about my work as more than a single transaction. I'm building relationships clients trust enough to call again, and to recommend to people they care about.

Does Kyara have access to off-market properties?

Yes. I have strong relationships with other agents across the Valley, which means I often know about off-market inventory before it's public. I also make it a point to connect directly with neighbors and people throughout the communities I work in, so I frequently know who's even thought about selling long before a home is anywhere near the market. Between my personal network and the connections we have at Williams Luxury Homes, I have a long list of solid options already in my back pocket, including new builds. And if I don't have exactly what a client is looking for, I'm determined to dig until I find it.

How does Kyara market a listing?

Marketing is one of my biggest strengths. I don't just list a home and wait, I go all in: social media, open houses, and events built around getting real attention on a property. Between my own reach and everything Williams Luxury Homes brings to the table, my listings get sustained visibility in front of serious buyers, not just a spot on the MLS.

Which communities does Kyara serve?

Kyara works with buyers and sellers throughout the greater Phoenix Valley, with her deepest focus in five communities: Paradise Valley, North Scottsdale, Central Scottsdale, Arcadia, and North Phoenix. Each has its own dedicated area guide covering pricing context, notable subdivisions, and lifestyle details. Browse all five communities. That said, she isn't limited to these five. Kyara is happy to work with buyers and sellers anywhere in the Valley, and her knowledge doesn't stop at these particular communities.
